✅ HIREPOS – How to Extract All Required Data from the UI

HirePOS provides most exports under:

Reports → Data Export
and
Main Menu → Lists (Customers, Items, Suppliers, etc.) → Export

Below is the breakdown for every entity you listed.


---

📌 1. Customers

Path:
Customers → Customer List → Export (CSV)

Contains:

Customer details

Addresses

Contact details

Pricing groups

Terms (if enabled)


If you need Payment Terms, export the customer list and also export the Terms list (see further below).


---

📌 2. Customer Ledger (Invoices + Payments)

HirePOS does not have a single “customer ledger” export, but you build it using:

A. Sales Invoices (Header & Lines)

Reports → Sales → Invoice Register → Export

Choose:

Detailed Export → includes line items

Summary Export → header only


B. Customer Payments

Sales → Payments → Export

This gives:

Payment date

Amount

Customer

Linked invoice numbers


You can join payments to the invoice register by Invoice Number.


---

📌 3. Sales Orders (Header & Lines)

Sales → Sales Orders → Export

Select Detailed for lines.


---

📌 4. Sales Invoices (Header & Line Items)

Same export as used for customer ledger:

Reports → Sales → Invoice Register → Export (Detailed)


---

📌 5. Purchase Orders (Header & Lines)

Purchases → Purchase Orders → Export

Choose Detailed to extract line items.


---

📌 6. Purchase Invoices (Header & Lines)

Purchases → Supplier Invoices → Export

Exports invoice lines & header data.


---

📌 7. Items / Products

Inventory → Products → Export

Exports:

Item codes

Descriptions

Pricing

Categories

Current stock levels (summary only)



---

📌 8. Item Ledger Entry (Stock Movements)

For real stock tracking history:

Reports → Stock → Stock Movements → Export

This includes:

Received

Sold

Adjusted

Transferred

Hire asset movements

Dates & quantities


This is the HirePOS equivalent of item ledger entries.


---

📌 9. Vendors / Suppliers

Suppliers → Supplier List → Export

Gets all supplier master data.


---

📌 10. Vendor Ledger Entry (Supplier Invoices + Payments)

HirePOS exposes supplier transactional data through:

A. Supplier Invoices

Purchases → Supplier Invoices → Export

B. Supplier Payments

Purchases → Payments → Export

Join together by Supplier Invoice Number.


---

📌 11. Payment Terms

Settings → Lists → Terms → Export

This gives the payment terms list used by customers + suppliers.


---

📌 12. Locations (Stock Sites)

Settings → Stock Locations → Export

Exports site/location names & codes.


---

📌 13. Currency

If multi-currency is enabled:

Settings → Currencies → Export


---

📌 14. Fixed Assets (Hire Equipment)

HirePOS treats hire assets as Items with Asset Tracking.

Export asset listing using:

Hire → Hire Assets → Export

OR if assets are linked to item master, export:

Inventory → Products → Export
(tick Include Assets)


---

📌 15. Manufacturers / Brands

These are under Item attributes.

Settings → Product Attributes → Brands / Manufacturers → Export
